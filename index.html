Profile

I have built cross-modal predictors from H&E slides for 21-gene recurrence-score risk groups (Scientific Reports 2025, first author), ER/PR/HER2 status (AACR 2023, first author) and gene expression (MoSPR, co-first author, 1st of 15 methods on gene-wise PCC in the paper's benchmark). A five-cancer study (GIW ISCB-Asia 2026, first author) then tested whether such predictions can replace the molecular assay. It applied one pre-registered protocol to all five cancers. Most endpoints that did not meet its confirmation criterion were reported as inconclusive rather than negative.

At OMIXAI I lead R&D on a multi-omics model that combines proteomics, RNA and H&E to predict drug response (in progress). At Deep Bio I built the WSI pipeline and contributed to pre-deployment model validation and regulatory documentation for a medical-AI product submitted to Korea's MFDS.

Preprint · 2026 · Under review · Co-first author †

MoSPR: Histology-to-Gene Expression Prediction with Morpho-Spatial Macrostates and Low-Rank Molecular Programs

A linear model that groups WSI patches into morphological microstates, finds spatial macrostates from their adjacency, and maps them to gene expression through low-rank molecular programs. It outperformed 14 baselines on three TCGA cancer types and stayed ahead of them when trained on less data. Because the model is linear, each prediction can be decomposed into global and region-specific programs.

Gene-wise PCCBRCA 0.413 · KIRC 0.334 · LUAD 0.358· 1st of 15 methods in the paper's benchmark

BIOINFO/GIW ISCB-Asia 2026 · Accepted poster · First & presenting author

Predictability is not substitutability: a cost-of-substitution framework for H&E-based molecular prediction across 5 cancers

Seoul · Nov 17–20, 2026. High accuracy does not make an H&E model a substitute for a molecular test. We defined substitution cost as the deviation from a prespecified treatment routing caused by each misclassification, and applied one pre-registered protocol (UNI + CLAM-SB, site-disjoint hold-outs, label-shuffle controls) to five cancers. Of about 15 endpoints, only HPV status in head and neck met the confirmation criterion. Most of the rest were reported as undecided rather than negative. With Pseudo Lab.

ConfirmedHNSC HPV · AUROC 0.959Excludedlung subtype · V(site, label) = 1.0

BIOINFO/GIW ISCB-Asia 2026 · Accepted oral · Co-author

A reliability map for per-gene multiome RNA velocity parameters in single-cell kinetics

Seoul · Nov 17–20, 2026. We asked whether the per-gene parameters from multiome RNA velocity methods (transcription rate α, degradation rate γ, chromatin-to-transcription lag) are reliable. Five velocity arms were tested on four axes (cross-method reproducibility, an ATAC-shuffle control, replication in five external multiomes, and comparison with measured synthesis and degradation rates). Only α reproduced across methods, and the γ and lag estimates need orthogonal validation.
